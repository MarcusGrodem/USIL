## One-paragraph summary

The research backbone is finished. All six country cases (Ghana, Botswana, Mauritius, South Korea, Malaysia, Philippines) have verified evidence files, the three original theories are fully tested against the evidence, and the shared source and claim registries are closed with no broken links. What remains is mostly production work: final page layouts, two outstanding figures, the colonial map, the final bibliography, the two interactive elements, and the physical/human review of the figure family before export.

## Where the project stands

### What is done

- **Scope and argument.** Research question, three theories, six countries, and three comparison years (1960, 1990, 2020) are locked.
- **Country evidence.** All six evidence files are controller-accepted. Remaining gaps are explicitly retained, not hidden.
- **Core dataset.** 131 rows passed source joins, unit checks, and display-rounding checks.
- **Economic and historical research.** Structural transformation, East Asian industrialisation, within-region variation, and the colonialism/borders/infrastructure/independence packs are all accepted.
- **Culture and theory.** Hofstede (with critical-use rules), the three theories, and the final theory-evidence matrix are accepted. 55 approved theory claims, 44 sources, 512 valid claim-to-source pairings.
- **Shared registries.** 97 sources and 292 claims, zero dangling links.
- **Typography.** Montserrat selected and propagated across all production figures.
- **Figures (digital).** The GDP dashboard, employment, manufacturing, and theory-matrix figures passed digital acceptance checks.
- **Design direction.** Spread A / Palette A chosen; 25-page rhythm plan accepted.
- **Photography shortlist.** 12 rights-verified candidates (two per country) registered.

## Current blocker and immediate next step

The project is waiting on **VIS-004**, the human / physical figure-decision review. Nine production figures have passed digital gates but have not yet been tested at actual A4 print size, under a five-second recall test, under a colour-vision check, and under a physical typography check. Until these are run on paper by a named reviewer, the figures cannot be marked publication-ready, and page writing is held behind them.

Everything else that is still missing can proceed in parallel once that gate clears.

## Honest limitations

- The old 45-page PDF is a storyboard. Numbers, maps, and causal claims from it are placeholders unless re-verified in the current source register.
- A single comparable six-country export-composition chart is not defensible; this is handled as narrative evidence rather than one chart.
- Direct trust and firm-size / informality measures are kept as narrative evidence, not as a common chart, because the constructs are not comparable.
- The 1914 colonial-control map and several historical visuals are still unmade.

## What a reader of the final magazine will get

A ~25-page magazine that asks why some African and Asian economies diverged, tests three original theories against six matched country cases, treats culture as one interacting factor rather than the cause, and separates verified evidence from interpretation throughout. Every figure uses matched definitions, years, and axes, and every claim is tied to a registered source.
