# DESIGN-001 draft page-rhythm plan

**Status:** REVIEW, direction and content gates remain open  
**Target:** 25 A4 pages, with a documented allowance for later reference or credit pages  
**Authority:** `SCOPE_LOCK.md` and `content/MAGAZINE_STRUCTURE.md`  
**Purpose:** test pacing, composition, and evidence visibility before any magazine-wide design is selected

## Gate legend

- **READY FOR LAYOUT TEST:** accepted evidence exists for the stated scope, but final copy, citations, rights, or visual-system selection may still be required.
- **PARTIAL:** some evidence exists, but a named dependency is missing.
- **GATED:** do not write factual final copy or build a final visual until the named research or rights task passes.
- **PLACEHOLDER ONLY:** a labelled box may test space and hierarchy, but it is not evidence and cannot survive into final pages.

No status in this plan approves final page copy, photography, maps, or a visual system.

## Rhythm principles

1. One page, one dominant job. Facing pages may form one argument, but neither page becomes a miscellany.
2. Every page supplies a five-second entry and a thirty-second evidence path.
3. The rhythm moves among chart, map, timeline, archival detail, question, interaction, diagram, and concise analysis.
4. A composition or main visual device is not repeated across three consecutive print spreads.
5. Real photographs are assigned an analytical role. Until rights and provenance pass, the plan shows only labelled placeholders.
6. Source, unit, date, gap, proxy, uncertainty, and causal limitation are part of the layout.
7. Pages 18 to 23 remain structurally planned but factually gated by `THEORY-001` and the relevant accepted evidence.

| Layer | Draft target at final A4 size |

| Dominant headline/question | 25 pt minimum, materially larger on openers |
| Deck / explanatory lead | 14 pt minimum |
| Body | 10.5 to 11.5 pt, 65 to 75 characters per line maximum |
| Chart axes and values | 11 pt minimum |

| Touch or write target | 9 mm minimum where a printed interaction expects a mark |
| Essential screen text | Dedicated 16:9 export; 22 to 24 px values/axes and 18 to 20 px supporting notes at 1280 × 720 |

These are planning targets, not verified final typography. The final typefaces remain unselected and require an A4 proof.
