## Editorial idea

Evidence Ledger is a chart-first editorial plate: one finding, one dominant evidence graphic, and one narrow evidence rail. It should feel investigative, precise, and visibly qualified rather than like a dashboard or an essay in a template.

The reader path is fixed:

1. **Five-second finding:** message headline, cases, unit, years, and dominant comparison.
2. **Evidence inspection:** common scale, direct labels, visible values, markers, gaps/proxies, and one or two decisive annotations.
3. **Qualification:** observed/proxy disclosure and “What this cannot prove.”
4. **Traceability:** concise APA figure note, source ID, and frozen-data path.

## A4 Evidence Ledger grid

Use A4 portrait pages (`210 × 297 mm`) and design paired spreads as a `420 × 297 mm` working canvas before imposition.

- **Page margins:** 14 mm outer/top/bottom; 16 mm inner margin at the fold.
- **Spread safety:** keep text and essential marks at least 10 mm from the fold; no value label may cross it.
- **Grid:** 12 columns across the spread, 5 mm gutters. Each page therefore contributes six columns.
- **Baseline rhythm:** 4 mm. Align titles, chart labels, rail modules, captions, and notes to it unless optical correction is documented.
- **Main evidence field:** columns 1–8, normally 66–69% of usable width.
- **Evidence rail:** columns 9–12, normally 31–34% of usable width.
- **Top band:** eyebrow, headline, and deck; target 36–52 mm depending on headline length.
- **Bottom source band:** full usable width; reserve at least 24 mm before body or chart layout begins.
- **Image role:** at most one supporting documentary image slot in the rail. It must never displace the source, limitation, or chart labels.

For a single page, collapse to a six-column grid and stack the evidence rail below the chart. Do not compress the rail into illegible marginalia.

## Palette A: selected data identities

Country colour identifies a case only. It never means success/failure, richer/poorer, modern/traditional, or a uniform regional path.

| Region | Country | Data colour | Marker | Required construction |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Africa | Ghana | `#B95332` | circle | fill plus charcoal keyline |
| Africa | Botswana | `#3F684E` | square | fill plus charcoal keyline |
| Africa | Mauritius | `#D6A23A` | up-triangle | fill plus mandatory charcoal keyline; connectors require charcoal under-stroke or a visible pattern |
| Asia | South Korea | `#315A78` | diamond | fill plus charcoal keyline |
| Asia | Malaysia | `#3E7568` | hexagon | fill plus charcoal keyline |
| Asia | Philippines | `#A63A3A` | cross | charcoal under-stroke plus thinner red cross |

Shared neutrals are cream `#F4F0E7`, charcoal `#252525`, dark neutral `#514E48`, and secondary grey `#B7B0A4`.

### Mandatory Mauritius repair

Mauritius ochre is 2.03:1 against cream and cannot form an essential edge by itself.

- Every essential ochre marker uses a charcoal outline of at least 1 pt at final A4 size.
- Every essential ochre line uses a charcoal under-stroke at least 1.5 times the ochre stroke width, or a documented colour-independent pattern with a directly adjacent label.
- Ochre is not used for body text, axis text, small text, or an unoutlined thin rule on cream.
- The country name, triangle marker, and value remain present in grayscale.

## Region navigation

Africa and Asia are navigation roles, not quantitative encodings.

- Use a written `AFRICA` or `ASIA` section label with a restrained warm or cool family cue.
- A region cue may appear in section tabs, folios, contents routes, or a short running rule.
- Do not recolour a country series to a generic continent colour.
- Do not use region colour as a proxy for cultural sameness or as a success/failure comparison.
- Whenever cases from both regions appear, country names and markers remain explicit.

## Separate national-accent layer

Flag-derived national colours are a presentation layer for country-led pages. They are not data colours and are not official flag reproductions.

Permitted roles are limited to:

- folios;
- a short headline detail or key word;
- caption tags;
- image frames;
- section tabs;
- short rules.

Use no more than two national accent colours prominently on one page, plus paper and ink neutrals. Verify contrast in the exact role.

National accents must not:

- recolour a chart, map, timeline, or comparison-data series;
- imply success, failure, direction, rank, or magnitude;
- replace direct country names or fixed markers;
- become cultural shorthand through decorative flag geometry, symbols, or repeated motifs.

## Direct labels and grayscale behaviour

- Put the fixed marker at every observed point and immediately beside every direct country label.
- Direct-label series at the endpoint when space permits; use a short leader only when collision requires it.
- For the focal pair, Ghana uses a solid connector and South Korea a long-dash connector. Other multi-series figures need documented line patterns if hue or luminance could converge.
- Never move a mark along the value axis to solve a label collision. Use cream halos, leaders, or a documented horizontal offset.
- In grayscale, identity must remain recoverable from country name, marker geometry, line pattern, and printed values. Luminance order is supportive, not authoritative.
- Legends may supplement dense figures but may not replace direct identification of the principal comparison.

## Reusable components

### Finding header

Eyebrow, message title, and factual deck. The deck always names the indicator, unit, cases, and actual years.

### Matched evidence plot

One shared geometry for all compared cases: identical definition, price basis, unit, years, dimensions, scale, and ticks unless a visible exception disqualifies direct comparison. Straight connectors may join observed anchors only; never smooth sparse observations.

### Country label chip

Marker + full country name + optional value. It uses the selected data colour only for the marker or short identifier, never for small body text.

### Evidence rail

Ordered modules: image placeholder if needed, observation/proxy disclosure, “What this cannot prove,” then short context. The causal limitation stays on the same spread as the major comparison.

### Image-rights placeholder

Use a neutral grey field with a 1 pt charcoal dashed boundary and the label `IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — NOT APPROVED`. Inside or immediately below, list the unresolved fields: country/place, subject, creator, date/period, source page, licence/rights URL, allowed crop/modification, required attribution, and analytical role. It is never a generic “Africa” or “Asia” image and cannot survive into final pages.

### Figure-note band

Separated by one charcoal rule. Include `Note.`, definition/unit, `Data from`, dataset and indicator, organisation/year, stable URL, retrieval/release information, source ID, and frozen-data path. A complete reference still belongs in the bibliography.

### Gap/proxy tag

Print the actual year beside the value. Use `NO COMPARABLE VALUE` for a gap and never connect across it. A proxy tag states the requested and actual year plus the visible reason.

### Projection companion

A separate 16:9 asset, not an automatic crop of an A4 figure. At `1280 × 720`, essential axes and values are at least 22 px, supporting notes at least 18 px, the limitation remains visible, and the full reference may move to a source slide or handout while the concise source stays on-screen.

## A4 and production constraints

- Keep essential content inside the trim-safe area and away from the fold.
- Minimum final chart/source type is 10 pt; axes and values are at least 11 pt.
- Essential strokes are at least 0.6 pt; keylines are at least 1 pt; focal data connectors are at least 3 pt.
- Markers are at least 3.5 mm across at final size.
- Use no transparency, gradients, shadows, 3D, dual axes, or decorative continent silhouettes as evidence.
- Test exports in colour and grayscale. A digital simulation does not equal a physical printer proof.
- Save the tidy data, editable source, publication export, chart specification, and verification record together.

## Release gate

Before magazine-wide propagation, each implementation still requires numeric/source verification, XML or export parsing, actual-size A4 inspection, grayscale inspection, contrast calculation, and a human five-second test. Physical A4 reproduction, human colour-vision review, and human classroom/back-row readability remain outstanding until named testers, dates, conditions, and results are recorded.
