Case one · Ghana

The lost three decades.

In 1960 Ghana was one of the richest new nations in Africa. Thirty years later its people were poorer than at independence. What happened between the flag and the graph?

GDP per capita · constant 2015 USD

Ghana's income per person in 1990 was 22% lower than in 1960. Recovery began in the mid-1990s. The 2020 level is only marginally above 1960's — six decades later.
Source: World Bank WDI (NY.GDP.PCAP.KD), pulled 2026-09-22.
