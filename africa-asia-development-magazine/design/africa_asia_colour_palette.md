## Africa/Asia navigation layer

Warm and cool families may cue explicitly labelled `AFRICA` and `ASIA` navigation in section tabs, folios, contents paths, and short running rules. Region colour never replaces the six country identities, recolours a country data series, or suggests that countries in one region followed one path.

## National-accent layer

Flag-derived colours may be used only on a country-led page, in folios, headline details, caption tags, image frames, section tabs, and short rules. Use at most two prominently on a page, plus the neutral paper and ink.

They may not recolour data series, imply success/failure or rank, replace country labels/markers, or become cultural shorthand through flag geometry or symbols. Check contrast and grayscale in the actual role.

Reference accent families: Ghana red/yellow/green; Botswana light blue/black/white; Mauritius red/blue/yellow/green; South Korea red/blue/ink/paper; Malaysia red/navy/yellow/white; Philippines blue/red/yellow/white. These are editorial cues, not official flag reproductions or quantitative encodings.

## Identification when colour is absent

Country name, unique marker, line pattern where needed, and printed value carry identity in grayscale. Colour is supportive. The selected mapping does not pass release solely because a digital simulation works; physical A4 reproduction, human colour-vision review, and human classroom testing remain open.
