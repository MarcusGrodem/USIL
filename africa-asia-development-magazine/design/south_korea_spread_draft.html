Case two · South Korea

From the same starting line.

In 1960 South Korea earned less per person than Ghana. It had been colonised, bombed and partitioned. Sixty years later its citizens were seventeen times richer. What did Korea do that Ghana did not?

GDP per capita · constant 2015 USD · same axis as Ghana

Korea earned $1,038 per person in 1960 — 5% less than Ghana. By 2020 the ratio was seventeen to one. The red dashed line is Ghana's 2020 level; Korea crossed it around 1979.
Source: World Bank WDI (NY.GDP.PCAP.KD), pulled 2026-09-22.
