\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Latest controller reconciliation:} 5 October 2026 (\texttt{CTRL-007}).

  \item \textbf{Final report ready:} No.
  \item \textbf{Work after reconciliation:} No newer specialist log or unreconciled deliverable was found.
\end{itemize}

\section{5. Research and APA progress}

\subsection{Country evidence}

All six bounded country evidence tasks are controller-approved: Ghana, South Korea, Botswana, Mauritius, Malaysia, and the Philippines. Each file addresses the required evidence categories and exposes proxy years, unavailable observations, and inference limits rather than silently filling gaps.

\subsection{Registry and bibliography coverage}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Shared registry coverage: Ghana and South Korea only.
  \item Registered sources: 17; registered claims: 89.
  \item Botswana, Mauritius, Malaysia, and the Philippines are not yet integrated into the shared registries.
  \item Full bibliography, independent citation audit, image-rights register, and map-source register are missing.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Comparability and data freeze}

The six-country comparison review and frozen master dataset are missing. The directories \texttt{data/master/} and \texttt{research/evidence\_packs/} do not yet exist.

Major unresolved issues include mixed WDI/WGI release vintages; incompatible export classifications; Philippines national-account manufacturing vintages; missing 1960 anchors; inconsistent informality and firm-size definitions; governance measures that cannot substitute for direct trust; historical literacy and electricity definition gaps; the pandemic-distorted 2020 endpoint; and missing thematic evidence on colonialism, industrialisation, Hofstede, and trust.

\section{8. Plain-language presentation summary}

\callout{jade}{\textbf{What the team has achieved.} Controller-approved evidence files now exist for all six countries, and Ghana and South Korea have a traceable source-and-claim system.\\[3pt]
\textbf{Where the project stands.} The project is 38\% complete and is moving from country research into comparison harmonisation and thematic research.\\[3pt]
\textbf{What happens next.} The team must freeze comparable data, extend the source registries, create the first verified comparison chart, and build the historical evidence pack.\\[3pt]
\textbf{Main risk.} Existing evidence uses different definitions, years, releases, and classifications. Until these are reconciled, much of the research cannot safely become comparative graphs, theory conclusions, or final magazine pages.}
