- **Decisions and limitations:**
    - **Layout decision.** Six panels in a two-row small-multiples grid were preferred to a single twelve-bar grouped-bar chart because the small-multiples layout keeps each country's two-year transformation visually contiguous and makes 1991-to-2020 shifts legible without a shared country colour. The grid obeys the chart rules for three-to-six-country comparisons.
    - **Region order.** Africa on Row 1 and Asia on Row 2 is navigation only. Both rows show internal variation — Botswana and Mauritius start from very different mixes within Africa; the Philippines holds a much larger agriculture share than South Korea or Malaysia within Asia — so the layout works against, not for, a race narrative. The reading-notes band and the `Not a race.` statement are explicit.
    - **Sector colour independence.** The sector fills are deliberately separated from the Palette A country colours. Country colour appears only in the panel-heading marker; the stacked segments identify the sectors in both colour and grayscale. This prevents accidental over-encoding (e.g., "Ghana red = agriculture").
    - **1960 exclusion.** No 1960 bar is drawn and no 1960 value is implied. The reading-notes band and the accessibility description both state that no 1960 observation exists in the ILO modelled three-sector series, so the 1960 anchor is excluded from this figure specifically.
    - **1991 proxy visibility.** Every 1991 bar is labelled `1991 †`; the panel foot and the reading-notes band both explain the symbol. No proxy year is silently relabelled 1990.
    - **Pandemic endpoint visibility.** Every 2020 bar is tagged `2020 pandemic-year endpoint` at the panel foot and the limitation is repeated in the reading-notes band.
    - **Deterministic display rule.** The dictionary's `× 10 / floor / allocate to largest fractional remainders / ties: agriculture → industry → services` rule is applied to every composition; the printed one-decimal values on the figure match the frozen master's `display_value_1dp` byte-for-byte and sum to exactly 100.0 in every bar.
    - **Full precision retained for calculation.** The output CSV preserves the frozen master `value` field exactly; the figure metadata restates the full precision; the segment heights were drawn from the display one-decimal values to guarantee the on-figure labels add up visibly, as required by the dictionary rule.
    - **No causal claim.** The figure does not claim that any factor caused any shift. The `What this comparison cannot prove` block names culture, policy, institutions, war, and aid as factors the chart cannot adjudicate between.
    - **Release scope.** All verification above is digital/numeric/geometric.
