**Status:** REVIEW  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-08  
**Cases:** Ghana, Botswana, Mauritius, South Korea, Malaysia, Philippines

## Decision in brief

These are **original group theories**, not established academic theories. The matrix tests bounded mechanisms rather than treating any theory as a proven explanation. The registered economic synthesis rejects cultural determinism and requires institutions, incentives, capabilities, resources, history, and international conditions to remain in the explanation ([ECON001-A07](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-MCMILLAN-RODRIK-VERDUZCO14-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-EAM93-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-RODRIK95-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-GHA20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-HILLBOM14-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-IMF-MUS14-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-NASUTION00-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WDI-001](source_registry.csv)).

## Non-negotiable evidence boundaries

- Hofstede dimensions remain archival, country-level, question-generating constructs—not individual traits, direct trust measures, timeless 2020 culture, or causes of growth ([RES002-A01](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-HOFSTEDE11-001](source_registry.csv); [RES002-A03](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-BREWER-VENAIK14-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-HOFSTEDE11-001](source_registry.csv); [RES002-A05](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-BEUGELSDIJK-WELZEL18-001](source_registry.csv); [RES002-A06](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-HOFSTEDE11-001](source_registry.csv)).
- No matched six-country Hofstede ranking exists: Botswana and Mauritius have no country rows, Ghana has only LTO and IVR, and regional rows cannot replace countries ([RES002-A02](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-HOFSTEDE-MATRIX-001](source_registry.csv)).
- Generalized trust, family/network trust, trust in named institutions, and governance perceptions are separate constructs ([RES002-A07](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-OECD-TRUST17-001](source_registry.csv)). WGI is perception-based governance with uncertainty, not trust, begins in 1996, and cannot be back-cast to 1960 or 1990 ([RES002-A08](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WGI26-001](source_registry.csv)).
- The Botswana, Mauritius, Malaysia, and Philippines direct-trust snapshots are mutually incompatible; Ghana and South Korea remain direct-trust gaps, and no 2019/2020 attitude is projected backward ([RES002-A09](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-AFRO-BWA21-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-AFRO-MUS21-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-IPSOS-MYS20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WVS-PHL24-001](source_registry.csv)).
- Employment, productivity, manufacturing value added, exports, and income are not interchangeable measures of transformation ([ECON001-A01](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-MCMILLAN-RODRIK-VERDUZCO14-001](source_registry.csv)). Manufactured exports do not by themselves measure domestic technological depth or value added ([ECON001-A06](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-NASUTION00-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-GHA20-001](source_registry.csv)); no common export-composition series is implied.
- Colonial inheritances combined assets and liabilities and did not dictate one path ([RES001-A12](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WB-GHA85-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-MAIPOSE08-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-HILLBOM14-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-IMF-MUS14-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-MUS-ARCH-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-XENOS70-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-KOHLI94-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-HAGGARD97-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-MYS24-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-DOLAN93-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-PHL87-001](source_registry.csv)).

## Theory 1 — Connected Development

### Mechanism

The bounded mechanism is that infrastructure contributes to productive transformation when it connects skills, investment institutions, firms, suppliers, workers, and markets; a route or access point alone can instead remain enclave-like or yield weak domestic spillovers ([MYS-THEORY-CONN](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WB-GHA20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-MYS16-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WDI-001](source_registry.csv); [PHL-THEORY-CONN](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-ADB-PHL07-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-NASUTION00-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WDI-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-BSP-BPO11-001](source_registry.csv); [RES001-A09](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-JEDWAB-MORADI12-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-GHA85-001](source_registry.csv)).

### Measurable prediction

The theory predicts that deeper domestic supplier, labour, skills, power, logistics, and market linkages should precede or accompany capability and productivity gains; rising access or gross exports without those linkages should coexist with weaker manufacturing deepening or local value capture ([MYS-THEORY-CONN](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WB-GHA20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-MYS16-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WDI-001](source_registry.csv); [PHL-THEORY-CONN](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-ADB-PHL07-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-NASUTION00-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WDI-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-BSP-BPO11-001](source_registry.csv); [ECON001-A06](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-NASUTION00-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-GHA20-001](source_registry.csv)). A future causal test would require dated network expansion, reliability/cost, firm and supplier linkages, and capability outcomes rather than endpoint access correlations ([BWA-ELC-2020](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WDI-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-BWA23-001](source_registry.csv); [PHL-ELC-2020](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WDI-001](source_registry.csv)).

Reject the theory as an important independent explanation if dated, comparable evidence shows that productive network depth consistently followed rather than preceded upgrading, or that similarly connected cases diverged after capabilities, institutions, ownership, shocks, and market access were accounted for ([MYS-THEORY-CONN](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WB-GHA20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-MYS16-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WDI-001](source_registry.csv); [PHL-THEORY-CONN](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-ADB-PHL07-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-NASUTION00-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WDI-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-BSP-BPO11-001](source_registry.csv)).

## Theory 2 — Radius of Trust, revised institutional/organisational-substitutes form

### Mechanism

The defensible mechanism is not “high-trust culture produces growth.” Cooperation beyond close networks may be extended through generalized expectations, reliable institutions, or organisational substitutes such as corporate hierarchy, foreign/state ownership, reputation, and repeated exchange; which channel matters must be measured ([RES002-A11](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-OECD-TRUST17-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-NUNN-WANTCHEKON11-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-IMF-MUS14-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-GHA20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-PSA-ASPBI23-001](source_registry.csv)).

### Measurable prediction

If the revised theory matters, historically matched measures should show that cooperation, delegation, contracting, or firm scale expands when generalized trust, named institutions, or identifiable organisational substitutes become stronger; low generalized trust should not prevent scaling where effective substitutes exist ([RES002-A07](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-OECD-TRUST17-001](source_registry.csv); [RES002-A11](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-OECD-TRUST17-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-NUNN-WANTCHEKON11-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-IMF-MUS14-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-GHA20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-PSA-ASPBI23-001](source_registry.csv)). WGI cannot serve as the trust measure in that test ([RES002-A08](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WGI26-001](source_registry.csv)).

### Strongest counterexample or alternative explanation

The Philippines is the strongest counterexample to the strong proposition that low generalized trust prevents organisational scale: scaling occurred, while the available trust observation is contemporary, unweighted, and incapable of identifying the historical channel ([PHL-THEORY-ROT](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WVS-PHL24-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WGI26-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-PHL80-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-PSA-ASPBI23-001](source_registry.csv); [PHL-TRUST-2019](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WVS-PHL24-001](source_registry.csv)). The main alternatives are courts, banks, corporate hierarchy, foreign/state ownership, networks, repeated exchange, policy, and market incentives rather than a single national trust trait ([RES002-A11](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-OECD-TRUST17-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-NUNN-WANTCHEKON11-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-IMF-MUS14-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-GHA20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-PSA-ASPBI23-001](source_registry.csv)).

### Limitation

The only harmonised six-country panel is WGI governance, not trust; the direct-trust observations differ by target, wording, sample/weighting, and year, and Ghana and South Korea have gaps ([RES002-A08](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WGI26-001](source_registry.csv); [RES002-A09](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-AFRO-BWA21-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-AFRO-MUS21-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-IPSOS-MYS20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WVS-PHL24-001](source_registry.csv)). Within-region variation defeats an Africa-versus-Asia trust ladder ([RES002-A10](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WGI26-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-AFRO-BWA21-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-AFRO-MUS21-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-IPSOS-MYS20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WVS-PHL24-001](source_registry.csv)).

### Condition that would revise or reject the theory

## Theory 3 — Continuity + Adaptation

### Mechanism

The bounded mechanism is that durable policy direction can give firms, workers, and institutions time to invest and learn, while performance feedback and correction prevent continuity from becoming permanent protection or rigidity ([ECON001-A03](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WB-EAM93-001](source_registry.csv); [MUS-THEORY-CONT](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-IMF-MUS14-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-MUS97-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-MUS19-001](source_registry.csv); [MYS-THEORY-CONT](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WB-GHA20-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-MYS16-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-MYS24-001](source_registry.csv); [PHL-THEORY-CONT](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-HILL13-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-ADB-PHL07-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-PHL87-001](source_registry.csv)).

### Measurable prediction

Sustained direction combined with explicit performance tests, correction, or sectoral adaptation should precede capability and productivity gains; continuity without discipline should coexist with rents, weak learning, or stagnation, while disruptive reversals should interrupt investment and learning ([ECON001-A03](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WB-EAM93-001](source_registry.csv); [ECON001-A04](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-WB-EAM93-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-RODRIK95-001](source_registry.csv); [PHL-THEORY-CONT](claim_registry.csv) → [SRC-HILL13-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-ADB-PHL07-001](source_registry.csv), [SRC-WB-PHL87-001](source_registry.csv)).
