# Six-country photography rights audit

**Task:** PHOTO-001  
**Audit date:** 2026-10-08  
**Status:** REVIEW  
**Scope:** Two viable documentary candidates for each locked country. This is a shortlist, not a final crop or page-image selection.

## Outcome

Twelve real, place-specific photographs are rights-verified and shortlisted: exactly two for Ghana, Botswana, Mauritius, South Korea, Malaysia, and the Philippines. The set balances everyday exchange and mobility with ports, industrial zones, and small-scale infrastructure. It does not use one country as a stand-in for another or treat an image as evidence of a causal claim.

Every viable item page and its displayed licence statement was opened on 2026-10-08. Creator, date, country/place, subject, source page, direct file URL, rights terms, attribution, permitted transformations, planned role, and limitations are recorded in `research/asset_rights_register.csv`. The new `SRC-PHOTO-COMMONS-*` labels identify sources awaiting later source-registry integration; PHOTO-001 was explicitly prohibited from editing the shared source registry.

## Rights and handling rules

- The twelve viable candidates use CC BY, CC BY-SA, or equivalent item-level permissions displayed on their Commons pages. Public accessibility alone was not treated as permission.
- Attribution must include the named creator, title/description, date, Wikimedia Commons (and Flickr where recorded), licence name/link, and an indication of cropping or other changes.
- For every CC BY-SA image, an adapted output must retain the same or a compatible ShareAlike licence. The final production team must check how this requirement applies to the exported page and separately reused image.
- Cropping, resizing, and restrained tonal correction are allowed under the recorded licences, but crops must preserve place and work context. No final crops were selected in this task.
- Several market and transport images include identifiable people, while item pages do not state model-release status. Use them editorially and contextually, avoid demeaning or isolating crops, and do not imply endorsement or sensitive personal claims.
- A photograph can supply context, atmosphere, or human scale. It cannot establish a country's culture, a causal mechanism, or the representativeness of the depicted scene.

## Unresolved

Its Commons page identifies the Jwaneng diamond mine and a NASA public-domain rationale, but the machine-readable/item summary does not identify a creator or capture date and the original NASA route is archived. It remains `UNRESOLVED`, not shortlisted.
- Model/property-release status is not stated on the shortlisted Commons item pages. This does not negate the recorded copyright licences, but final editorial use must avoid endorsement or sensitive-person implications.
- Final page placement, crop, colour treatment, print reproduction, and classroom-screen performance remain for production review.

- Reject using a photograph to prove culture, trust, productivity, policy effectiveness, industrial capability, or causal development outcomes.
