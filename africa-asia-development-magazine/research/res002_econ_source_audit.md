## Required locator rechecks

### World Bank (1993)

The official World Bank PDF and catalogue metadata were reopened. The registered locators preserve the accepted scope:

- pp. 4–7: shared fundamentals alongside heterogeneous policy mixes;
- pp. 20–26: export-performance discipline, technology access, selective intervention, mixed results, and the inability to construct rigorous counterfactuals;
- pp. 79–80: international conditions;
- pp. 127–130: Korea's policy/credit/export sequence and heavy-industry pressures;
- pp. 159–161: land/agricultural change, education/infrastructure, and correction;
- p. 242: targeting costs and failures.

The source is an authoritative comparative synthesis, not a causal decomposition or proof of one East Asian formula.

### Rodrik (1995)

The final publication metadata are confirmed as *Economic Policy, 10*(20), 55–107, DOI `10.2307/1344538`. The exact accepted statement—initial exports were too small for the claimed aggregate causal role and coordinated/subsidised investment is a more plausible initial-acceleration account—is verified in the abstract of NBER Working Paper 4964, which links to the 1995 publication. No final-page locator was invented. The source row is therefore `PARTIAL`, and the claim locator says “NBER abstract.”

### Hofstede (2011)

The full article supports the country-versus-individual level warning at pp. 6 and 8, the six definitions across pp. 9–16, the wealth-control warning at p. 8, and the wealth-to-individualism reverse-direction warning at p. 16. These are country-level question-generating constructs, not individual traits, trust measures, timeless observations, or causal estimates.

### McSweeney (2002)

The opened final article supports the sampling, selected IBM occupational frame, organisational/occupational matching assumptions, national-uniformity problem, and causal-overreach critique across pp. 93–114. It is registered as a methodological limitation, not as proof that all aggregate cultural comparison is invalid.

### OECD (2017)

The official PDF distinguishes generalized/limited interpersonal trust from institutional trust and shows that institutional measures must name their target. The registered locators also preserve wording, scale, population, context, mode, nonresponse, and interpretation limits. This source does not authorise a common numeric scale for the four country snapshots.

### Nunn and Wantchekon (2011)

The final AER item supports differentiated trust targets in Table 3 (p. 3237) and IV results in Table 6 (p. 3241). It is historical African evidence, not a matched six-country trust index and not permission to back-cast a national score.

### Official Hofstede matrix

The official unmodified CSV is version 2015-12-08. Its header is `ctr;country;pdi;idv;mas;uai;ltowvs;ivr`. Checked rows are `AFE`, `AFW`, `GHA`, `KOR`, `MAL`, and `PHI`. Ghana has only `LTO=4` and `IVR=72`; Botswana and Mauritius have no rows. `AFE` and `AFW` are explicitly regional rows. Malaysia's raw PDI is 104, further showing that the original scale is comparative and not a natural bounded 0–100 measurement.

## Construct and comparability gates retained

- Hofstede scores remain archival, country-level, incomplete, and question-generating. Missing rows/cells are not zero and are not imputed from regional aggregates.
- WGI remains a perception-based governance composite with uncertainty, not generalized, institutional, family, court, bank, or business trust.
- Botswana court trust, Mauritius political-institution trust, Malaysia bank/business-leader trust, and Philippine unweighted WVS cases remain separate narrative cards. Ghana and Korea direct-trust gaps remain visible.
- No 2019/2020 attitude is projected to 1960 or 1990.
- Employment, productivity, MVA, export composition, and GDP per capita retain separate meanings.
- Ghana's early MVA observation remains visibly labelled `1965 proxy`; employment begins in 1991; 2020 remains visibly identified as a pandemic year.
- Historical authority categories, SITC/HS/national groups, denominators, service coverage, re-exports, and national-account vintages remain incompatible. Narrative export evidence cannot become a common chart series.
- No accepted row treats correlation, an endpoint gap, a policy sequence, or a cultural score as a standalone causal estimate.

## Approved

- Registry coverage is complete for all 18 accepted pack claims.
- The ten new APA/source records and 18 new claim records are structurally complete and join without dangling links.
- Eight new sources are fully verified. The two partial sources are sufficient only at their explicitly bounded uses and have not been upgraded.
- The WGI/trust boundary, Hofstede level-of-analysis boundary, direct-trust incompatibility, export-series prohibition, proxy years, pandemic anchor, vintage limits, and causal limits are all present in the shared rows.

## Revision required

- No registry-coverage revision is required before THEORY-001 begins.
- Before final magazine publication, any wording attributed specifically to Brewer and Venaik beyond the publisher abstract requires full-text access and a checked internal page locator.
- Before final magazine publication, Rodrik's position must continue to be cited as located in the NBER working-paper abstract unless the corresponding final-article page is independently verified.
- Hillbom and the two PSA records remain partial-access supports. They must stay paired with opened official/quantitative sources and may not carry a unique precise statistic or causal conclusion.

## Unresolved

- A harmonised, weighted, close-year six-country direct-trust dataset is not available; Ghana and Korea direct-trust evidence remains missing.
- Historical trust near industrial take-off remains unavailable and contemporary snapshots cannot be back-cast.
- A common six-country shift-share decomposition, matched historical skills panel, and comparable firm-learning/supplier-depth evidence remain unavailable.
- A common export series remains blocked pending one saved classification, code map, FOB denominator, retrieval record, and re-export rule for every country/year.
- Historical Philippines MVA remains incompatible with the current WDI vintage; Ghana 1960 MVA remains unavailable and the 1965 proxy must stay explicit.

## Rejected

- Regional Hofstede rows as substitutes for Ghana, Botswana, or Mauritius.
- Hofstede country scores as descriptions of individuals, organisations, fixed 2020 culture, direct trust, or causes of growth.
- WGI as trust, or WGI/direct-trust values on one numeric scale.
- A single cross-country scale for Botswana, Mauritius, Malaysia, and Philippines trust snapshots, including the Philippine 5.3% unweighted case share as a population estimate.
- Any back-cast of 2019/2020 trust to 1960/1990.
- A six-country manufactured-versus-commodity chart or connected trend assembled from incompatible narrative sources.
- “Ghana did not structurally transform,” “Korea grew because it exported,” “industrial policy caused the East Asian miracle,” or “Africa could not grow like Asia because of culture.”

## Verdict on THEORY-001

**Registry coverage is sufficient to start THEORY-001.** The theory owner may use these 18 approved rows only at their registered scope and must carry forward the partial-access, construct, timing, proxy, classification, pandemic-year, and causal limitations. SRC-004 does not unblock any common trust chart, common export-composition series, score-based cultural ranking, or deterministic cultural explanation.
